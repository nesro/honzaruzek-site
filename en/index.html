About me

My work makes a real difference

Sport has been part of my whole life, and early on it was clear it would become my career. My passion for movement led me to study at the Faculty of Physical Education and Sport, Charles University, where I specialised in sports training and movement diagnostics. Today I turn that passion into helping others on their way to better health, fitness and lifestyle.

I have worked as a trainer and nutrition coach for 7 years, coaching clients from all over the world in the Czech Republic and in Dubai. In that time I have trained more than 3,000 hours. My training is built on scientific evidence combined with hands-on experience.

Every plan is tailored to you – your goal, fitness, health and daily routine. I know life gets hectic, so I adapt to you.

  • Master’s degree, Charles UniversitySports training and movement diagnostics
  • 8 certificationsNASM Understanding Fitness Programming · Coretraining strength training Level 1 and 2 · IQ Pohyb – core stabilisation, posture diagnostics, scoliosis · Institute of Modern Nutrition – effective weight loss
  • Experience in Prague and DubaiClients from beginners to athletes and people recovering from injuries
